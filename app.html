Pack Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works out whole packs, even splits and pack costs from the counts and prices you type. It assumes the sizes and prices are what the store really charges. It does not know about sales, coupons, tax or what is actually in stock. Check those at the shelf or the checkout.
