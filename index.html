Pack Math

Need 12 and they come in 8s? You buy 16 and 4 are left over.

Boundary: arithmetic only Pack Math works out whole packs, even splits and pack costs from the counts and prices you type. It assumes the sizes and prices are what the store really charges. It does not know about sales, coupons, tax or what is actually in stock. Check those at the shelf or the checkout.

The buy truth

Needing 12 in packs of 8 takes 2 packs, which is 16 in all with 4 left over.

The even truth

14 split among 4 groups gives 3 each with 2 left over. Adding 2 (16 in all) gives 4 each; taking away 2 (12 in all) gives 3 each.

The compare truth

Needing 24: A (packs of 6 at 4.50) takes 4 packs for 18.00, with none left over; B (packs of 10 at 7.00) takes 3 packs for 21.00, with 6 left over. A costs 3.00 less.

Run your numbers
Pack Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter, nothing more.
